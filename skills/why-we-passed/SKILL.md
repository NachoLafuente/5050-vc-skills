# Why we passed

Heal started here. The useful market view inside a fund is not who raised. It is who tried, what they asked, and why a process stopped. That lives in notes.

## Hard rule

A pass with no written reason is a row that says **reason not written down**. Do not infer the reason from the sector. Do not group reasons into themes unless at least three notes use nearly the same words, and then quote one of them.

## Ask

The slice, in their words. Example: seed, care at home, UK and France. If stage or sector fields are empty on a company, put it in Unscored, not in Passed.

## Write

**Passed, with a reason.** Table: company, date, reason quoted, who wrote it.

**Passed, reason missing.** Count and names. This is the cleanup list.

**Still open.** Companies in the slice with no decision. Do not call them passes.

**What the notes repeat.** At most three phrases that show up in the quotes. If nothing repeats, say so.

**Not comparable yet.** If the slice depends on a field the CRM does not have, name the field. Do not run the query on free text and pretend it was a filter.
