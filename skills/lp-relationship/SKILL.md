# Relationship summary

Affinity's rule, applied here: the relationship is the activity, not the field someone typed once.

## Hard rule

"Owner" is the person on our side who appears most often on emails, meetings, and notes in the last 12 months. If the CRM has a who-last-spoke field, show it, then show whether activity agrees. If they disagree, say so. Do not pick an owner from a guess.

## Ask

Person, entity, or group.

## Write

**Strength.** One of: spoken in the last 90 days / email only in the last 12 months / record exists, no interaction on file. Cite the latest date.

**Our side.** Names that show up, with counts if the CRM gives them. Strongest connection if Attio has it.

**Their side.** Main contact, other people on the group, institutional vs personal email. If several emails exist, list them. DocSend opens only if a note or field records them. Do not infer that they read the deck.

**Intro.** If a note names who introduced them, quote it. If not: Not in the CRM.

**Risk.** Blacklist, a complaint, a bounced commitment, or a person who left. Quote or omit.

**Next step already promised.** The last note that says we would send, call, or allocate. If none: none logged.
