# Re-up brief

When a16z Crypto raised its first fund, the LP conversation led with what could go wrong, so the people who came in had opted into the risk. Use that shape for a re-up. The brief is for the partner walking in. It is not the letter.

## Hard rule

Their history comes from the LP history skill's rules: CRM only. Fund performance (DPI, TVPI, IRR) appears only if it is already in Attio or in a document the user pasted in this chat. If it is not, write **Performance not in the materials provided.** Do not recall a number from memory or from the web.

## Ask

The LP group, and the vehicle you want them to consider.

## Write

**What they already did.** Funds, amounts, last interaction. From the CRM.

**What we are asking.** Vehicle, target check, close timing. Only what the user just said. Do not invent a target.

**What could go wrong.** Three downside points. Each one must be either in a note, in the user prompt, or marked as a question the partner still has to answer. No generic venture risks copied from a blog.

**What they complained about last time.** Quote or "no complaint on file."

**Ask for the room.** One sentence the partner can say. No superlatives. No "excited to partner."
