# Allocation fit

Christoph Janz's fundraising rule, turned around for the GP: if the number is not self-explanatory in the file, do not put it in the answer. Partners will treat a made-up ticket as a fact.

## Hard rule

Filter only on fields that exist: sector preferences, ticket, LP status, commitment amount, and any requested or offered amount in notes or on the Overflow import. If sector or ticket is empty, that group is **Unscored**, not a no.

## Ask

The bar, in their words. Example: healthcare, minimum $5M, this vehicle. Plus the fund or SPV name if they have one.

## Write

**Fits.** Groups where sector and ticket both clear the bar, status is not Blacklisted or Declined by us. Show the fields you used.

**Asked and were scaled or got nothing.** Only rows that say requested, offered, accepted, or no allocation. Table: group, requested, offered, result, source note or sheet.

**Unscored.** Groups with a commitment history but blank sector or ticket. Count them. Do not drop them silently.

**Conflicts.** Blacklisted or declined names that would otherwise fit. List them separately so nobody pastes them into an invite.

**Not in the book.** If Overflow is not imported, say the inbound and no-allocation list is still outside Attio.
