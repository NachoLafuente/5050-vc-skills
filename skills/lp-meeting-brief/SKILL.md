# LP meeting brief

Pattern: Affinity Meeting Prep (brief from the CRM before the call; nothing is written back until a human says so). Adapted for an Attio IR book: Investor Group → Entity → Person.

## Hard rule

Every line cites a field, a note, or an email already in the workspace. If it is not there, write **Not in the CRM.** Do not guess ticket, sector, AUM, or who owns the relationship.

## Ask

Name the person, the legal entity, or the investor group.

## Read, in this order

1. Person: name, emails, role, who last spoke, DocSend sent, deck version, last email, last calendar, Granola note if linked.
2. Entity: legal name, country, domicile, investor type, AUM, blacklist and reason.
3. Investor Group: LP status, status reason, sector preferences, ticket, linked entities and people.
4. Commitments: fund, vehicle, amount, currency, class, status. One row per commitment.
5. Notes and emails in the last 18 months, newest first. Quote the sentence. Date it.

## Write this, and stop

**Who.** One line. Person, entity, group.

**Last touch.** Date, channel, who from our side, one sentence of what was said. If none: Not in the CRM.

**Money on file.** Table of commitments. Separate line for anything marked passed, declined by us, or blacklisted, with the reason field verbatim.

**What they asked for vs what we gave.** Only if allocation, requested amount, or a note says so. Otherwise: Not in the CRM.

**Open thread.** The last unanswered ask from them, or from us. If the last note has no ask: say that.

**Do not raise.** Blacklist reason, a pass reason, or a complaint in notes. Quote it.

**Three questions for this call.** Only questions the record cannot already answer.
